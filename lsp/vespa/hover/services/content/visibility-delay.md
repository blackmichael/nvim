## visibility-delay

Contained in [search](https://docs.vespa.ai/en/reference/services-content.html#search). Default 0, max 1, seconds.

This setting controls the TTL caching for [parent-child](https://docs.vespa.ai/en/parent-child.html) imported fields. See [feature tuning](https://docs.vespa.ai/en/performance/feature-tuning.html#parent-child-and-search-performance).
