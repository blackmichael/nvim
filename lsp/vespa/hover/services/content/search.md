## search

Contained in [content](https://docs.vespa.ai/en/reference/services-content.html#content), optional. Declares search configuration for this content cluster. Optional sub-elements are [query-timeout](https://docs.vespa.ai/en/reference/services-content.html#query-timeout), [visibility-delay](https://docs.vespa.ai/en/reference/services-content.html#visibility-delay) and [coverage](https://docs.vespa.ai/en/reference/services-content.html#coverage).
