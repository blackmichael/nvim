## cluster-controllers

Container for one or more [cluster-controller](https://docs.vespa.ai/en/reference/services-admin.html#cluster-controller) elements. When having one or more [content](https://docs.vespa.ai/en/reference/services-content.html) clusters, configuring at least one cluster controller is required.
[Read more](https://docs.vespa.ai/en/reference/services-admin.html#cluster-controllers)
